# **GREEN VALLEY PARK – BLENDER ENVIRONMENT GENERATION PROMPT**

# **PART 2 — CHILDREN'S PLAYGROUND (DETAILED MODELING SPECIFICATION)**

---

# PLAYGROUND OVERVIEW

Create a premium municipal children's playground located in the **northwest quadrant of Green Valley Park**. The playground should appear professionally designed, safe, spacious, and suitable for children aged **3–12 years**. The design should match the reference image, with colorful equipment, rubber safety surfacing, landscaped edges, and open circulation around the play structures.

The playground occupies an area approximately **26 meters (east–west) × 22 meters (north–south)**.

It is surrounded by low hedges and flower beds, but remains visually open to the rest of the park.

The playground should be divided into four functional zones:

1. Main Play Tower
2. Climbing Zone
3. Swing & Activity Zone
4. Toddler Play Zone

Maintain at least **2 meters of clear safety space** around all equipment.

---

# PLAYGROUND FOUNDATION

The entire playground sits on a perfectly level concrete slab.

Dimensions:

* Width: 26 m
* Length: 22 m
* Thickness: 200 mm

Above the slab install a continuous EPDM rubber safety flooring.

---

# SAFETY FLOORING

Material:
High-density EPDM rubber.

Thickness:
50 mm.

Color layout should be playful but organized.

Create large flowing shapes instead of random patches.

Suggested color distribution:

* 35% Bright Blue
* 25% Green
* 20% Yellow
* 20% Orange

Avoid sharp geometric transitions.

Blend colors using large curved forms.

The flooring extends beneath every piece of equipment with a **minimum 2-meter safety buffer** beyond the equipment footprint.

# MODELING RULES

* Keep all equipment as separate Blender objects for easy editing.
* Apply realistic bevels to all exposed edges (3–10 mm depending on part size).
* Use smooth shading on curved components and auto-smooth where appropriate.
* Ensure no geometry intersections between equipment, flooring, or landscaping.
* Name objects consistently (e.g., `PlayTower_Main`, `Slide_Primary`, `MonkeyBars_01`, `SwingSet_A`, `SpringRider_Horse`, `Bench_Playground_01`).
* Maintain child-safe rounded corners on all interactive elements.
* Leave sufficient circulation space between play structures for pedestrians and maintenance access.
