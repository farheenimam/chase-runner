# **GREEN VALLEY PARK – BLENDER ENVIRONMENT GENERATION PROMPT**

## **PART 1 — MASTER SITE LAYOUT, TERRAIN, BOUNDARY, ENTRANCE, JOGGING TRACK & MAIN AXIS**

---

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

Create a highly detailed, photorealistic modern public park environment named **Green Valley Park**. The design should resemble a premium municipal park located in the center of an urban neighborhood. The park should feel organized, luxurious, spacious, family-friendly, and suitable for real-world construction. Every object must follow realistic architectural proportions and engineering standards. Avoid exaggerated fantasy elements; instead, aim for a clean, elegant, and practical design.

The overall environment should be modeled as a rectangular site with a clear central axis. The entrance is located on the southern edge, facing a main boulevard. A straight promenade runs through the center of the park, terminating at a circular fountain plaza. The park is divided symmetrically into left and right zones, creating a balanced landscape. Every path, tree, bench, and planting area should align with this axial composition. The terrain is completely flat with no slopes or elevation changes.

---

# OVERALL SITE DIMENSIONS

Construct the site as a rectangle measuring approximately **90 meters wide (east to west)** and **120 meters long (south to north)**.

Use Blender units where **1 Blender Unit = 1 meter**.

The park occupies the entire site inside the decorative boundary fence.

Reserve a 2-meter landscape buffer between the inside of the fence and the jogging track.

The jogging track forms the first circulation ring inside the fence. Landscape beds fill the remaining space between the track and the fence.

---

# TERRAIN

Create one continuous terrain mesh with no visible seams.

Dimensions:

* Width: 90 m
* Length: 120 m
* Thickness: 0.4 m

The terrain should be perfectly level. Do not create hills, depressions, berms, or uneven ground.

Top surface should consist of:

* Lawn areas
* Walkways
* Flower beds
* Playground safety flooring
* Fountain plaza
* Gazebo foundation

Edge bevel:

* 40 mm chamfer

Subdivision:

* Moderate density to allow smooth landscaping transitions.

---

# GROUND MATERIALS

Grass areas should occupy approximately **65% of the park**.

Grass characteristics:

* Short ornamental turf
* Uniform height (40–50 mm)
* Healthy medium green
* Dense coverage
* No visible dirt patches
* Slight variation in blade orientation for realism

Walkways, plazas, and hardscape areas occupy the remaining **35%**.

Maintain perfect symmetry for all major hardscape elements.

Open lawns should remain uncluttered, ensuring clear sightlines and flexible recreational space.

---

# MODELING GUIDELINES

* Keep all geometry clean and manifold.
* Use real-world proportions for every asset.
* Separate major elements into individual Blender collections (Terrain, Fence, Gate, Promenade, Jogging Track, Landscape, Lighting, Furniture).
* Maintain consistent object origins and naming conventions.
* Use modular assets for repeating elements such as fence panels, benches, lamp posts, and columns.
* Ensure paths meet cleanly at intersections with no overlapping geometry.
* Leave adequate space for the fountain, playground, gazebo, and future landscape features described in subsequent parts.
