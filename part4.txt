# LANDSCAPE PHILOSOPHY

The landscaping should create the feeling of a mature, professionally maintained municipal park. Every planted area must have a purpose. Trees provide shade, shrubs define circulation, flower beds create focal points, and open lawns remain uncluttered. Avoid randomly scattering vegetation. Every planting group should be organized into layers: ground cover, shrubs, flowering plants, and canopy trees.

The entire park should feel lush but not overcrowded.

---

* Boxwood
* Indian Hawthorn

* Viburnum

Arrange in flowing masses rather than straight rows.

* Marigold

* Lavender
* Salvia
* Begonia

# MODELING RULES

* Use real-world scale throughout.
* Apply bevel modifiers to all hard edges.
* Avoid overlapping geometry.
* Keep topology clean and quad-based where possible.
* Use instancing for repeated assets (trees, benches, lamp posts, fence panels).
* Maintain proper pivot points at the base center of each object.
* Apply transforms before exporting.
* Separate static and movable assets into different collections.
* Use high-quality PBR materials with consistent texel density.
* Maintain realistic proportions and construction logic for every structure.

# FINAL ENVIRONMENT GOAL

The completed scene should feel like a **real, buildable municipal park** rather than a fantasy environment. Every path, tree, bench, structure, and planting bed should have a logical purpose and placement. The composition should guide visitors naturally from the entrance through the central promenade to the fountain, while the playground, gazebo, open lawns, and jogging track provide balanced spaces for recreation, relaxation, and community use. The overall aesthetic should be elegant, highly organized, richly landscaped, and suitable for presentation as a professional architectural visualization or Blender environment.
