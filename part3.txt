---

# CENTRAL PARK ORGANIZATION

The central portion of the park serves as the primary gathering space and visual anchor. It is organized around a large circular fountain plaza connected by a straight promenade from the main entrance. From this plaza, secondary walkways radiate toward the playground, gazebo, lawns, and jogging track. The design should emphasize symmetry, open sightlines, and easy pedestrian circulation.

The fountain plaza should feel spacious and elegant, acting as the heart of the park where visitors naturally gather, rest, and orient themselves.

---

# CENTRAL FOUNTAIN PLAZA

## Overall Layout

Position the fountain plaza exactly at the intersection of the park's central north–south axis and the east–west circulation axis.

Overall plaza diameter:
**20 meters**

Shape:
Perfect circle.

Surface:
Premium natural stone paving.

The paving pattern should be radial, with concentric circular bands surrounding the fountain.

The entire plaza must be perfectly level.

---

Height:

# MODELING RULES

* Maintain clean topology with separate objects for fountain tiers, basin, benches, gazebo columns, roof, paths, and planting beds.
* Apply realistic bevels to all exposed edges.
* Ensure all paths connect seamlessly with no overlapping geometry.
* Use modular assets for benches, lamp posts, and planters to simplify scene management.
* Keep the central axis unobstructed, preserving clear views from the entrance to the fountain.
* Name objects consistently (e.g., `Fountain_Base`, `Fountain_Tier01`, `Gazebo_Roof`, `Bench_Fountain_01`, `Tree_Maple_03`, `Path_East_01`) for an organized Blender project.
